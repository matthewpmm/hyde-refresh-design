## In-Room Text Translations
Though there has been a deliberate attempt to minimize this, in a few places throughout the room there are English-language text clues and journal entries that are important to understanding the story and puzzles of the room. To ameliorate this for foreign-language speakers, we will be implementing an optional system via the accessibility tablet. When players would gain access to a piece of text that might be useful to have translated, an image containing a translated version will appear on the accessibility tablet.
