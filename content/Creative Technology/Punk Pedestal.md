# Deliverables
- One completed prop with a [COGS RFID master and readers](https://run-on-cogs.myshopify.com/collections/rfid-master) installed. We can provide the tech for you to install.
# Design Specifications
- This should be a comfortable height for players to place the puzzle jars on top of and move them around.
- The pedestal must have a door locked with a [cabinet lock](https://a.co/d/04qQTBF0) on the front face.
- There must be an internal compartment right under the top surface accessible to maintenance and installation staff but inaccessible to players. This will hold the tech.
- There must be a hole in the back of the cabinet through which cables can be run to the internal tech.
- The correct positions to place the jars should be marked with either an elevated platform or a recession.
- Any paint on the top surface of the pedestal should be covered with a layer of resin to better resist wearing off from friction over time.
# Technical Specifications
- The wires for the internal cabinet lock must be terminated in JST connectors. All internal wires should be concealed, and ideally inaccessible to players.
