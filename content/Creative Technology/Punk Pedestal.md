A small table on which the [[Props/Puzzle Jars]] will be placed in the correct positions to solve the Pickled Punks puzzle.
# Deliverables
- One completed prop with a [COGS RFID master and readers](https://run-on-cogs.myshopify.com/collections/rfid-master) installed and concealed, as well as a cabinet locked with a controllable electronic [cabinet lock](https://a.co/d/04qQTBF0). We will provide the tech for you to install.
# Design Specifications
- This must be a comfortable height for a player of average adult height to place the puzzle jars on top of and move them around.
- This must be appropriately sized to fit inside the [[Creative Technology/Pickled Punks Tent]] while still leaving room for the [[Pickled Punk Shelf]] and for players to comfortably stand inside and interact with the jars and pedestal. It must also be large enough for all four [[Props/Puzzle Jars]] to sit comfortably on top of.
- The pedestal must have a door locked with a [cabinet lock](https://a.co/d/04qQTBF0) on the front face.
- There must be an internal compartment directly under the top surface accessible to maintenance and installation staff but inaccessible to players. This will hold the tech.
- There must be a hole in the back of the cabinet, or it must be easy to drill a hole, through which cables can be run to the internal tech.
- The correct positions to place the jars should be marked with either an elevated platform or a recession.
- The surface the jars are intended to be placed on to trigger the RFID readers should be no more than 1/2" thick, and must have enough open surface area underneath to support mounting of a [Blue RC-522 RFID reader](https://run-on-cogs.myshopify.com/products/blue-rc-522-rfid-reader-1m-jst-cable-mifare-tag).
- Any paint on the top surface of the pedestal should be covered with a layer of resin to better resist wearing off from friction over time.
# Technical Specifications
- The wires for the internal cabinet lock must be terminated in 2-pin JST connectors. All internal wires should be concealed, and ideally inaccessible to players. (The locks we will provide are already terminated )
