[[Puzzles/Puzzle 3 - Pickled Punks]] will take place inside a large tent in the [[Rooms/Courtyard]]. The tent itself will need to be constructed, and a heavy canvas fabric will need to be sourced.
# Deliverables
- One fully framed tent measuring ~6' wide x 6' deep x 8' tall that can be assembled inside the existing experience and attached securely to the walls and floor.
- Full assembly of the tent inside the experience.
# Design Specifications
- The tent must be themed to complement the [existing circus tent](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/17_amLmX4zwB1vz07TuSuUE0OPJnrqfoB?usp=drive_link).
- The tent framing should be constructed almost as though it were a physical room rather than a functional tent designed to be broken down.
- The tent fabric must be a tough material resistant to player meddling, such as a heavy canvas.
- The tent must be designed in such a way as to make it possible to screw the tent directly into the adjacent walls and concrete floor.
- Please coordinate with the decorator responsible for the [[Pickled Punk Shelf]] and the [[Creative Technology/Punk Pedestal]] to determine if it is necessary to add additional supports to which either or both of those furniture items can be attached.
# Technical Specifications
- 3 [cabinet locks](https://a.co/d/0fQzu2hg) secured to the frame must be used to hold the cloth door of the tent closed. The cabinet locks should be concealed from view or interaction with by players.
