A dressed-up dummy the size of a small human. She clutches a small purple crystal ball in one hand and clutches the bars of her cage with the other. Her mouth is covered with a scarf or bandana. Her head moves slightly via a servo motor while she talks.
# Deliverables
- One fully operational Contortionist dummy with animatronic head, fully functional and ready to be connected to electrical hardware.
- A fully sourced bill of all materials and electronic parts used for future maintenance purposes.
# Design Specifications
- The figure should look kind of ghoulish and emaciated. She has been frozen in time and trapped in this cage for unknown decades.
- This character should be creepy, but also somewhat sympathetic. She cannot resemble a clown.
- She must have a [[Props/Throwing Knives\|throwing knife]] sticking out of her, clearly visible when the [[Lighting/Limelight]] is pointed at her in [[Puzzles/Puzzle 6 - Wheel of Death]].
# Technical Specifications
- The Contortionist's head must be able to rotate slightly to make her appear more lifelike by using some sort of servo-driven mechanism. These servos should be able to be attached to external COGS hardware by JST connectors.
