# Overview
This is one of the centerpiece set pieces of the entire experience. It is a large spinning wheel with a corpse attached to it.

- The Star
- The Crescent Moon
- The Big Top
- The Ringleader
- The Lion
- The Clown
- The Acrobat
- The Tall Man
- The Animals
- The Eye
- The Specimen
- The Contortionist
- The Damned
# Carpentry
#carpentry
Dimensions: 5' diameter wheel, elevated 1-2' above the floor by its stand
This does NOT have to be a perfect circle. In fact, if it looks a little rough and hand-cut around the edges, that might even be preferable to a perfect circle, provided the edges are properly sanded and finished.
