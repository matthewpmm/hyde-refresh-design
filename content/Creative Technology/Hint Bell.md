In this experience, pre-recorded hints spoken by [[Creative Technology/The Contortionist]] can be accessed by ringing one of two bells. These bells are fully functional and make noise on their own, but are also connected to COGS electronically and complete a circuit similar to a simple switch or button to inform the system that a hint has been requested.

One of these will go on the table in the [[Rooms/Boiler Room]] next to the [[Creative Technology/Contortionist's Crystal Ball]], and the other will be mounted underneath the [[Creative Technology/The Contortionist]]'s cage in the [[Rooms/Courtyard]].
# Deliverables
- Two fully functional hint bell props with electrical outputs ready to be connected to COGS.
# Design Specifications
- The bell that sits on the table in the [[Rooms/Boiler Room]] should be an ornate, classic looking desk bell, similar to [this item](https://www.etsy.com/listing/826874490/antique-style-brass-service-bell-hand).
- The bell for the [[Rooms/Courtyard]] should be a very classic, almost stereotypical looking dome-shaped bell. It can also be very ornate to match the other bell.
# Technical Specifications
- The "Clapper" (the part of the bell that strikes the dome of the bell) and the dome of the bell should both be able to conduct electricity.
- The "Clapper" should also be electrically isolated from the dome.
- This should essentially function like a switch - when the conductive clapper comes into contact with the dome, it should complete a circuit between the two and the change should be detectable by a digital input.
- All wiring should be ROBUSTLY attached to the conductive pieces of the bell.
- The wires connected to each component should connect to a female [3-pin JST connector such as these](https://a.co/d/0cz6UqxK), with the terminals of the switch connected to either of the two outermost contacts (the middle contact will remain unused).
