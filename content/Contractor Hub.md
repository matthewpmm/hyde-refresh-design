# Projects Available for Bids

**Our goal is to have all of these projects completed by April 19.**

We are currently accepting BIDS for the following projects, to be delivered prior to the installation period. Right now, most of the linked pages are empty, but full specifications/design briefs will soon be provided to clarify the scope, deliverables, and expectations. For the time being, if you'd like to see what each item looks like in context, I recommend searching for it in the [[Script\|latest draft of the script]].

Many of these are projects that will require tech/electronics knowledge, although they exist on a spectrum between very low tech (e.g. the Limelight) and quite high tech (Gas pipe puzzle/Gas Lantern). Most of these, however, only have a few simple sensors or lights in them and are primarily mechanical to a large degree.

We will provide specifics for exactly how the tech should be set up so that it can be easily integrated into the COGS show control system we are planning to use. Most of the show logic will be programmed with COGS, so most of these props will just need a few basic components installed to function properly. Only one or two will potentially require programming as a deliverable, and these are likely to involve more in-person work and direct oversight from our team.

If you don't have a lot of confidence in your own electronics skills but any of these projects still sound interesting to you, we will also entertain bids on just the fabrication of the physical elements and will install the tech elements in-house or find another contractor for those elements.

Many of these items will require prep and finishing work for painting but not the painting itself, as we will be hiring 1-2 scenic painters to paint all the finished props to ensure a consistent look and feel throughout the entire experience.

Don't forget to check out the [Pinterest mood board](https://www.pinterest.com/matthewpmm/hyde-circus-refresh/) for the project to get a sense for the overall vibe and aesthetic of the experience!

# Payment Information and Contractor Agreement
## Payment

The final sale cost for each project or bundle of projects will be negotiated in advance and written into a personalized contract for each contractor. For projects greater than $1,000 in value, we are willing to discuss advance or installment payments.

For hourly contractors, the hourly rate will also be negotiated in advance and written into a personalized contract for each contractor.

Payment will be mailed via check or transmitted via ACH transfer (as requested) no more than 3 business days after project delivery, or no more than 5 business days after of the last day of work for each hourly contractor.

All contractors will be required to go through a short onboarding procedure through Awkward Dog Interactive's Gusto payroll system. This will involve filling out a few short tax forms and signing your contract.
## General Contract Terms for Project Contracts

For the language below, the Client is defined as Awkward Dog Interactive LLC, and the Contractor is the specific contractor in question.

- Each contract will have a clear list of measurable deliverables (indicated on the project page) specifying the conditions the finished item(s) must meet. If the item(s) is delivered without meeting all of the deliverables specified in the contract, the Contractor agrees to forfeit a proportional fraction of the final balance owed by the Client based on the number of deliverables not met. For example, if 10 deliverables are specified but only 9 are fulfilled on delivery, 10% of the owed balance is forfeit.
- For every calendar day after the agreed upon due date that the finished item(s) is delivered, 3% of the total sale cost will be deducted from the balance owed by the Client.
- The Client will be entitled to 1-3 progress reviews (depending on the complexity or criticality of the specific project item(s)) before the due date, at which time they may give notes and request specific changes that do not alter the agreed upon deliverables specified in the original agreement.
- After signing the contract, the Contractor will have seven calendar days in which they may voluntarily terminate their contract (in writing) and forfeit the agreed upon payment balance with no additional fees or penalties. After seven calendar days have passed since the agreement is signed, the Contractor will be held responsible for delivering the finished item by the due date.
- The Contractor will be required to provide a 6 month warranty policy for any custom built props.
