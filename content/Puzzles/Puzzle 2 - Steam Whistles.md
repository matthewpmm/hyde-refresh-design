- 6 [[Creative Technology/Steam Whistle]]s mounted in the rafters of the [[Rooms/Passenger Car]]
- [[Props/Newspaper Clipping]]
# Puzzle Specifications

Spread out around the rafters of the train car are 6 [[Creative Technology/Steam Whistle]]s, from which hang pull chains with decorative sculpted handles. Each of the pull chain handles depicts a different circus act, animal, or exhibit. Pulling any of these chains by the handle triggers a whistle to begin blowing a unique pitch; releasing the handle triggers it to stop.

For internal and programming numbering purposes, the whistles are numbered from 1-6 in order of how far away each is from the entrance door; the closest to the door is #1 and the furthest from the door is #6.

The handle of each whistle from 1-6 depicts each of the following subjects:

1. Elephant trunk
2. Magic wand
3. Contortionist
4. Jar
5. Knife
6. Eyes

Inside the [[Props/Tarot Table]], guests find a [[Props/Newspaper Clipping]] from an article about the upcoming reopening of Twilight Circus and the changes coming to it.

Throughout the text of the clipping, specific circus acts, display pieces, and animals are mentioned by name in the following order:

1. Elephant
2. The Contortionist (mentioned twice)
3. Pickled Punks
4. The Illusionist
5. The Contortionist (mentioned twice)
6. The Knife Thrower
7. Eye of the Ancient One

To solve the puzzle, guests must draw the connection between the order of the seven things mentioned and the subjects depicted on the six whistle pull chain handles, determine the correct order of the seven acts, and pull the chain with a handle depicting each act in the order above.

The matching handle subject for each of the acts mentioned is as follows:

| Whistle Number | Handle         | Act/Animal/Exhibit     |
| -------------- | -------------- | ---------------------- |
| 1              | Elephant trunk | Elephant               |
| 2              | Magic wand     | The Contortionist      |
| 3              | Contortionist  | Pickled Punks          |
| 4              | Jar            | The Illusionist        |
| 5              | Knife          | The Knife Thrower      |
| 6              | Eyes           | Eye of the Ancient One |

In terms of the whistle numbers, the solution order is 1342356.
