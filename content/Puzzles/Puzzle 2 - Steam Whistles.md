# Puzzle Specifications

Spread out around the rafters of the train car are 6 [[Creative Technology/Steam Whistle]]s, from which hang pull chains with handles. Each of the pull chains has a tag on it depicting a different exhibit added to the circus. After the [[Broken Valve + Valve Handle]] is used to allow steam to flow through the pipes, any of these chains by the handle triggers a whistle to begin blowing a unique pitch; releasing the handle triggers it to stop.

For internal and programming numbering purposes, the whistles are numbered from 1-6 in order of how far away each is from the entrance door; the closest to the door is #1 and the furthest from the door is #6.

The tag on each whistle chain from 1-6 depicts each of the following exhibits:

1. Jackalope
2. Fiji Mermaid
3. Musical Monkey
4. Rat King
5. Kraken Egg
6. Eye of the Ancient One

Before beginning the puzzle, the [[Broken Valve + Valve Handle\|valve handle must be rotated inside the broken valve]] to allow steam to flow through the whistles.

A [[Transit Map]] is secured to the table in the train car. There are a number of marked and labeled train stops on the map. Six of these spots correspond to the symbols on the whistle chain tags.

Upon careful observation of the [[Train Ticket]] given to guests, the back side of the ticket has a series of arrows drawn between the hole punches.

When the ticket is laid over the map arrow-side up, it can be positioned such that each of the marked train stops is neatly visible through each hole in the ticket. The arrows then indicate the correct order in which to pull the whistles.

Correct order to pull the whistles (by number): 1342356
Correct order to pull the whistles (by exhibit): Jackalope, Musical Monkey, Rat King, Fiji Mermaid, Musical Monkey, Kraken Egg, Eye of the Ancient One
