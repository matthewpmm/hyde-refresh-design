A relatively simple, but not effortless, introductory puzzle involving several set pieces in the [[Rooms/Passenger Car]] to get momentum going.
# Relevant Items
- [[Creative Technology/Contortionist's Crystal Ball]]
- [[Props/Hyde Circus Reopening Posters]]
- Tarot Table
- 10 tarot cards
# Puzzle Specifications

Displayed on the base of the [[Creative Technology/Contortionist's Crystal Ball]] is a series of four moon phase symbols, arranged in a horizontal sequence:

![[Media/Crystal Ball Glyphs v2.png]]

We will refer to these moon symbols as moons 1, 2, 3, and 4, ordered from left to right.

One of each of these moons can be found stamped onto one of each of the four [[Props/Hyde Circus Reopening Posters]] spread around the walls of the passenger car.

- Moon 1 is stamped on the poster of the **[[Creative Technology/Wheel of Death]]**.
- Moon 2 is stamped on the poster featuring **two performers** that reads "Not for the faint of heart!"
- Moon 3 is stamped on the poster featuring an **[[Props/Acrobat]]** in a ring that reads "The Greatest Show Around"
- Moon 4 is stamped on the poster featuring the **[[Props/Eye of the Ancient One]]** that reads "Beware! The Ancient Eye Sees All"

A total of 7 highly durable, custom-fabricated [[Tarot Cards]] containing NFC tags are found spread throughout the room. These cards are:

- 0. The Fool
- I. The Magician
- VI. The Lovers
- IX. The Hermit
- X. Wheel of Fortune
- XII. The Hanged Man
- XIX. The Sun

The Tarot Table underneath the window next to the courtyard exit door has four clearly indicated rectangular spaces arranged from left to right on which to place tarot cards.

![[Media/IMG_5940.jpg]]

To solve the puzzle, guests must first determine the correct order of posters based on the order of the moons stamped on them corresponding to the left-to-right order of moons on the crystal ball. They must then identify the four tarot cards from the available set that correspond most closely to each of the four posters and arrange them on the Tarot Table in the left-to-right order determined prior.

The tarot cards corresponding to each poster are as follows:

- The poster of the Wheel of Death corresponds to the **Wheel of Fortune**.
- The poster featuring two performers that reads "Not for the faint of heart!" corresponds to **The Lovers**.
- The poster featuring an acrobat in a ring that reads "The Greatest Show Around" corresponds to **The Hanged Man**.
- The poster featuring the Eye of the Ancient one that reads "Beware! The Ancient Eye Sees All" corresponds to **The Sun**.
## Solution:

|                | Tarot Table 1    | Tarot Table 2  | Tarot Table 3   | Tarot Table 4          |
| -------------- | ---------------- | -------------- | --------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Moon**       | 1                | 2              | 3               | 4                      |
| **Poster**     | Wheel of Death   | Two Performers | Acrobat in Ring | Eye of the Ancient One |
| **Tarot Card** | Wheel of Fortune | The Lovers     | The Hanged Man  | The Sun                |
# Build Specifications

- Poster stamps
	- Stamps should be positioned slightly overlapping the primary subject of each poster
	- Stamps should use permanent ink and be clearly visible on each poster, contrasting with the poster colors sufficiently to stand out in dimmer light
- RFID Tarot Cards: see the [[Tarot Cards]] page for more information.
