A relatively simple, but not effortless, introductory puzzle involving several set pieces in the [[Rooms/Passenger Car]] to get momentum going.
# Relevant Items
- [[Creative Technology/Contortionist's Crystal Ball]]
- [[Props/Hyde Circus Reopening Posters]]
- Tarot Table
- 10 tarot cards
# Puzzle Specifications

Printed on the base of the [[Creative Technology/Contortionist's Crystal Ball]] is a series of four abstract glyphs, arranged in a horizontal sequence:

![[Media/crystal ball code.jpg]]

We will refer to these glyphs as glyphs 1, 2, 3, and 4, ordered from left to right.

One of each of these glyphs can be found stamped onto one of each of the four [[Props/Hyde Circus Reopening Posters]] spread around the walls of the passenger car.

- Glyph 1 is stamped on the poster of the **[[Creative Technology/Wheel of Death]]**.
- Glyph 2 is stamped on the poster featuring **two performers** that reads "Not for the faint of heart!"
- Glyph 3 is stamped on the poster featuring an **[[Props/Acrobat]]** in a ring that reads "The Greatest Show Around"
- Glyph 4 is stamped on the poster featuring the **[[Props/Eye of the Ancient One]]** that reads "Beware! The Ancient Eye Sees All"

A total of 10 (highly durable, custom-fabricated) tarot cards containing NFC tags selected from the Major Arcana of the [Rider-Waite tarot deck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rider%E2%80%93Waite_Tarot) can be found stacked on the Tarot Table underneath the window next to the courtyard exit door. By default, these cards are:

- 0. The Fool
- I. The Magician
- VI. The Lovers
- VII. The Chariot
- IX. The Hermit
- X. Wheel of Fortune
- XII. The Hanged Man
- XIII. Death (this may need to be swapped out to avoid confusion with The Sun)
- XX. Judgement
- XIX. The Sun

The Tarot Table has four clearly indicated rectangular spaces arranged from left to right on which to place tarot cards.

To solve the puzzle, guests must first determine the correct order of posters based on the order of the glyphs stamped on them corresponding to the left-to-right order of glyphs on the crystal ball. They must then identify the four tarot cards from the available set that correspond most closely to each of the four posters and arrange them on the Tarot Table in the left-to-right order determined from the glyphs on each poster.

The tarot cards corresponding to each poster are as follows:

- The poster of the Wheel of Death corresponds to the **Wheel of Fortune**.
- The poster featuring two performers that reads "Not for the faint of heart!" corresponds to **The Lovers**.
- The poster featuring an acrobat in a ring that reads "The Greatest Show Around" corresponds to **The Hanged Man**.
- The poster featuring the Eye of the Ancient one that reads "Beware! The Ancient Eye Sees All" corresponds to **The Sun**.
## Solution:

|                | Tarot Table 1    | Tarot Table 2  | Tarot Table 3   | Tarot Table 4          |
| -------------- | ---------------- | -------------- | --------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Glyph**      | 1                | 2              | 3               | 4                      |
| **Poster**     | Wheel of Death   | Two Performers | Acrobat in Ring | Eye of the Ancient One |
| **Tarot Card** | Wheel of Fortune | The Lovers     | The Hanged Man  | The Sun                |
# Build Specifications

> [!NOTE]
> We will fabricate the entire Major Arcana (including two additional duplicates of each card that is part of the correct solution) and keep unused cards on-hand as backups. All will contain RFID readers and be functional on the Tarot Table in the event a last-minute swap is necessary.

- RFID Tarot Cards
	- Fabrication
		- Card shapes cut into thin acrylic with a CNC router
		- Front and back card art sourced from high quality Rider-Waite tarot card scans and printed onto sticker paper
		- Thin NFC tag adhered to the back of each card and programmed in COGS
		- Card art adhered to each side of the acrylic.
		- Entire card coated in resin to increase durability
	- Cards to print:
		- CORRECT PUZZLE SOLUTIONS (3x each)
