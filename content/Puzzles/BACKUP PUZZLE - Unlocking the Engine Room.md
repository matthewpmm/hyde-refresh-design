> [!NOTE]
>  This puzzle has been CUT from the intended room plan. However, it would be relatively inexpensive to construct and add back in with minimal changes to the existing structure should the experience be determined during playtesting to require additional content. 

Once players return to the train, they find that the door to the engine room is still locked. A [[Creative Technology/Engine Room Lock\|grid of unmarked metal buttons]] is arranged on the face underneath the [[Props/Engine Room Sign]].

When players shine the now-blacklight [[Creative Technology/Gas Lantern]] on the door to reveal how to open it, they find an invisible painting of a darkened new moon painted on the face of the door, indicating the completely un-illuminated jet black moon visible in the courtyard.

If players return to the courtyard and re-insert the lantern into the [[Lighting/Limelight]], the magnified light becomes a blacklight as well. Pointing the spotlight at the new moon reveals a hidden symbol, showing the order in which to press the buttons on the engine room door to unlock it.
