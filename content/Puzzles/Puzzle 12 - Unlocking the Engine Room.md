# Relevant Items

# Puzzle Specifications

A painted outline on the [[Rooms/Engine Room]] door can be observed to strongly resemble the tentacles of the [[Props/Eye of the Ancient One]]. Placing the eye on this spot triggers the door to suddenly spring open.
# Build Specifications

- We will need to add at least one reed switch to the back of the engine room door, centered on the painted outline.
- This was not originally specified in the documentation for the [[Props/Eye of the Ancient One]], but for this puzzle we will need to insert a suitably strong magnet into the base of the prop, directly behind the eye in the center of the tentacles.
