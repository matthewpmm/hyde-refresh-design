# Notes
Twilight Circus is a blend of several different aesthetic styles, including:
1. Late 1800s technology, culture, and graphic design
2. Typical circus-themed tropes
3. The darker, grislier elements of circus sideshow performances designed to shock and titilate audiences of the time with morally questionable exhibits and perfomances
4. Cosmic/lovecraftian horror with minimal body horror elements (especially in the fleshy flourescent "eye growths" seen throughout)
**Please note that there are NO CLOWNS in this experience.** Many audience members have a specific phobia of clowns, and while this is a spooky circus theme, it is one that explicitly does not involve clowns or many of the grindhouse/slasher horror tropes associated with clowns over the past few decades (as seen in Stephen King's *It*, the *Terrifier* series, *Killer Klowns from Outer Space*, etc.).
