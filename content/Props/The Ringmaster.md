The former leader of the circus. His desire to maintain the popularity and status of the circus led to him turning it into a sideshow and acquiring the haunted artifact, the [[Props/Eye of the Ancient One]], which led to the circus becoming trapped in this nightmare world on the night before its reopening. Eventually, after becoming trapped in the nightmare world, his performers turned on him and strung him up by his bullwhip inside the big top.
# Deliverables
- One fully decorated Ringmaster dummy.
- A securely attached "bull whip" prop from which the Ringmaster can be suspended from the ceiling by his neck.
# Design Specifications
- The Ringmaster will be accessible by players, and must be built robustly and with all clothing and decoration securely attached to the dummy so it cannot be removed. (Players really like disrobing dummies.)
- There must be a place to attach the [[Ringmaster's Brooch]], which is an interactable prop that players will remove during the course of [[Puzzles/Puzzle 12 - Obtaining the Eye]]. (We are open to packaging this prop in with this contract as well.)
- The portion of the Ringmaster's bull whip that extends beyond his neck must have at least 8 feet of slack.
- The ringmaster must be clutching his neck and have died with a look of horror on his face to emphasize that he was murdered, and did not hang himself.
# Performance
- The ringmaster only has one line of dialogue.
