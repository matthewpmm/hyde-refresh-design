Late 1800s posters advertising the then-upcoming reopening of Hyde Circus and its new attractions.
# Deliverables
- Research on three potential vendors who can manufacture copies of each unique design on canvas, vinyl, or another rugged, guest-proof medium.
- Original print-quality digital design files for 5 separate Hyde Circus reopening posters subject to two rounds of revision each. Each poster should specifically feature an advertisement for one of the following set pieces/performers featured in the actual experience:
	- [[Creative Technology/The Contortionist]]
	- [[Creative Technology/Wheel of Death]] (throwing knife wheel)
	- [[Props/Eye of the Ancient One]]
# Design Specifications
- The reopening date of the attraction should be specified as April 7, 1891.
- None of the posters may use AI-generated art.
- The posters must be in the style of classic print illustration or black and white print photography styles using stock images or illustrations as the key images.
- All posters must be made in the style of late 1800s circus sideshow posters, or posters for similar attractions, using the original Hyde Circus posters as a point of reference.
- In the lore of this experience, unlike the original Hyde Circus, the reopening was supposed to be a lurid sideshow, something more scandalous, shocking, and controversial. This should be captured in the designs.
- The name of the fictional circus must still be indicated to be Hyde Circus in-universe, although the name of the escape room experience in real life will be different.
- The final set pieces the poster designs should be based on may not be completed by the time these designs need to be delivered, but please coordinate in the Discord with the fabricator/decorator assigned to each item to make sure the stock images or illustrations chosen more or less line up with the design intent for each set piece or character.
# Technical Specifications
- All design files should meet the following specifications:
	- Color format: CMYK
	- 300dpi
	- Dimensions: approximately A2 (16.5x23.4")
- The final files should be compatible with the templates or provided specifications required by whichever manufacturer we choose, even where those differ from the ones specified above.
# Reference Images
You can and should use the original Hyde Circus poster decorations as a point of reference! But make sure to make them look more creepy, weathered and desaturated.

Please refer to the primary [Pinterest mood board](https://pin.it/1rie3ujax) for style inspiration for classic circus posters.
