Variants on the other Pickled Punks that are used to solve [[Puzzles/Puzzle 4 - Pickled Punks]] by placing them in the correct position on the [[Creative Technology/Punk Pedestal]].
# Deliverables
- Four [[Props/Puzzle Jars]] with unique RFID tags, and four spares of each puzzle jar identical to the original ones.
# Design Specifications
- The jars must be extremely robust and difficult for players to break or open.
- If the jars contain any kind of liquid, they must be even more robust and impossible for players to open.
- The jars chosen or the color of the "liquid" inside should look different from the other [[Props/Pickled Punks]] to communicate their interactability and significance as puzzle elements to players.
- The contents of each jar should be clearly visible and discernable.
- Description of each specific jar with additional specifications:
	- Jar 1: Punctured Lung
		- This jar should contain a single lung with multiple visible puncture wounds. A foam throwing knife should be pierced through it, serving as a hint for [[Puzzles/Puzzle 6 - Wheel of Death]].
		- The lung should ideally be a bit large, and might look good if squeezed into the jar and bent over somewhat.
		- A tongue would also make sense in this jar.
	- Jar 2: Six-Fingered Hand
		- This jar should contain the bones of a single hand with six fingers on it.
	- Jar 3: Teeth
		- This jar should contain a copious number of human teeth. Some of the teeth should be rotten, and several of them should be gold.
		- This jar should contain a bit of glitter to make it satisfying to shake around and look for details inside. We want guests to have to search inside and notice the gold teeth to infer that it belongs to the Benefactor.
	- Jar 4: Shrunken Heart
		- The main item in this jar should be an anatomically correct human heart that is small, withered, and blackened as though by soot.
		- Any supplemental contents of this jar should suggest that it belongs to an evil person.
# Technical Specifications
- A 13.56MHz NFC tag compatible with [Blue RC-522 readers](https://run-on-cogs.myshopify.com/products/blue-rc-522-rfid-reader-1m-jst-cable-mifare-tag) should be securely fastened to the bottom interior surface of each jar, and should also be concealed from players. We will provide compatible NFC tags.
