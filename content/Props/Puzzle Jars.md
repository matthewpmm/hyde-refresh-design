Variants on the other Pickled Punks that are used to solve [[Puzzles/Puzzle 3 - Pickled Punks]] by placing them in the correct position on the [[Creative Technology/Punk Pedestal]].
# Deliverables
- Four [[Props/Puzzle Jars]] with unique RFID tags, and four spares of each puzzle jar identical to the original ones.
# Design Specifications
- The jars must be extremely robust and difficult for players to break or open.
- If the jars contain any kind of liquid, they must be even more robust and impossible for players to open.
- The jars chosen or the color of the "liquid" inside should look different from the other [[Props/Pickled Punks]] to communicate their interactability and significance as puzzle elements to players.
- The contents of each jar should be clearly visible and discernable.
# Technical Specifications
- A 13.56MHz NFC tag compatible with [Blue RC-522 readers](https://run-on-cogs.myshopify.com/products/blue-rc-522-rfid-reader-1m-jst-cable-mifare-tag) should be securely fastened to the bottom interior surface of each jar, and should also be concealed from players. We will provide compatible NFC tags.
