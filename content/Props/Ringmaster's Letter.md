The players receive this letter from The Ringmaster inviting them to board the Hyde Circus Express to see the grand reopening of Hyde Circus.

> [!NOTE] Note to Fabricator
> The intro sequence may be changed slightly, so please hold off on this for a short while.
# Deliverables
- 1-10 fully fabricated samples of the Ringmaster's Letter, and following approval:
- 500 fully fabricated copies of the Ringmaster's Letter.
- The final versions of all digital image or design files used to fabricate the prop.
- A written guide (with photos if relevant) to fabricating the prop from scratch for the purpose of future reproduction.
# Design Specifications
- These must be designed for mass production, as there is no way to guarantee guests won't take these home.
- This should be a small rectangular letter and envelope (~4"x3").
- The stationary used should look relatively ornate and very, very classic (circa late 1800s/early 1900s).
- The text should not be just a computer font - it should either be actually written by hand or written with relatively realistically simulated human handwriting. Ideally, this should be cursive or cursive-like while still being as clear and readable as possible.
- The prop should contain all text specified below. If this text differs from the version in the [[Script (Draft 3)\|Script]], the version in the script takes precedence.
## Text

> *April 6, 1891
> 
> Dearest guests,
> 
>I am delighted to extend a most cordial invitation to the grand reopening of Hyde Circus, the oldest and most fantastic amusement venue in this hemisphere. I have been hard at work acquiring many new attractions to whet your appetite for mystery and novelty.
> 
> Please make your way to the Hyde Circus Express and climb aboard to begin your journey.
> 
> I simply cannot wait to dazzle and amaze you.
> 
> — The Esteemed Ringmaster of Hyde Circus
