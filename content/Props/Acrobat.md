A dressed-up human dummy decorated to look like a deceased acrobat suspended from the sky by their aerial silks. They have been frozen in time by the magic of this dimension.
# Deliverables
- One fully decorated Acrobat dummy.
- Securely attached fabric resembling aerial silks that are strong enough to hold the dummy permanently.
# Design Specifications
- They must be fixed in a highly physical pose with a striking silhouette.
- They cannot resemble a clown.
- For safety and longevity purposes, the dummy must be as light as possible.
- The dummy must be built with any additional support materials necessary to prevent it from sagging over time.  
- They must have a [[Props/Throwing Knives\|throwing knife]] sticking out of them, clearly visible when the [[Lighting/Limelight]] is pointed at them in [[Puzzles/Puzzle 6 - Wheel of Death]].
