A canvas sign affixed near the [[Creative Technology/Gas Pump]] to instruct players on how to interact with the puzzle.
# Deliverables
- ~~Research on three potential vendors who can manufacture the sign on some sort of thin metal such as aluminum.~~
- An original print-quality digital design file for the sign subject to two rounds of revision each.
# Design Specifications
- This must look time-period appropriate.
- All text and images on the sign should be highly visible in low lighting.
- This may not use AI-generated art.
- Must convey the following using pictographs/icons/illustrations:
	- Gas canisters must be inserted into the pump cap-down.
	- When gas canisters are inserted into the pump, gas will flow through the pipes.
	- The pipe network connects the pump to the Pickled Punks tent, the Lantern Cage Mechanism, and the Wheel of Death, and unlocks the door to the circus tent.
# Technical Specifications
- All design files should meet the following specifications:
	- Color format: CMYK
	- 300dpi
	- **Dimensions: TBD**
- The final files should be compatible with the templates or provided specifications required by whichever manufacturer we choose, even where those differ from the ones specified above. (We will likely print this sign in-house.)
