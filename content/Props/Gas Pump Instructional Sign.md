A metal sign affixed to the front of the [[Creative Technology/Gas Pump]] to instruct players on how to interact with the puzzle.
# Deliverables
- Research on three potential vendors who can manufacture the sign on some sort of thin metal such as aluminum.
- An original print-quality digital design file for the sign subject to two rounds of revision each.
# Design Specifications
- This must look time-period appropriate.
- All text and images on the sign should be highly visible in low lighting.
- This may not use AI-generated art.
- Must clearly feature the text "GAS PUMP" somewhere on the sign.
- Must convey the following using pictographs/illustrations:
	- Gas canisters can be attached to connectors in a specific orientation.
	- When gas canisters are attached to the connectors, gas will flow through the pipes.
	- When the valve is turned, it switches between outputting gas to the three puzzles and merging all three gas canisters into one larger output.
# Technical Specifications
- All design files should meet the following specifications:
	- Color format: CMYK
	- 300dpi
	- Dimensions: TBD
- The final files should be compatible with the templates or provided specifications required by whichever manufacturer we choose, even where those differ from the ones specified above.
