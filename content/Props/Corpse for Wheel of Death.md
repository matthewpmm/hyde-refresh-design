A desiccated corpse or skeleton speared through with a throwing knife.
# Deliverables
- A fully decorated corpse or skeleton dummy ready to be attached to the Wheel of Death. 
- Attachment of the corpse to the Wheel of Death.
# Design Specifications
- The corpse should be slightly shorter than the diameter of the [[Creative Technology/Wheel of Death]].
- The dummy's hands and feet must be tied together and securely attached to the top and bottom of the wheel, oriented similarly to how the skeleton is in the first reference image.
- The corpse may be wearing clothes, but does not have to be.
- The dummy may slightly flop around while rotating on the wheel, but must be attached both at the center of the wheel and the hands and feet.
