# Build Specifications
The wheel should be about 6 feet in diameter and able to turn freely via a controllable motorized mechanism. This mechanism should be concealed so players cannot injure themselves or break the mechanism.

The wheel only needs to be thick enough to to insert the blade of each throwing knife.

The primary build contractor is not responsible for painting, but should mud and sand all surfaces to prepare for painting and finishing work.
