For [[Puzzles/Puzzle 5 - The Limelight]], we will need a light with handles that can be rotated to point a DMX spotlight around the room. This "Limelight" will be aimable by players, and can be activated by placing the [[Creative Technology/Gas Lantern]] inside a compartment in the base.
# Deliverables
- One completed, fully functional limelight prop containing a DMX controllable spotlight
- A maintenance guide explaining how to access the internals
# Design Specifications
- We are flexible on the specifics of the design. We would like to see your take on the specific design for this prop.
- The base should stand about 3' tall.
- The top of the light should be about 4' above the ground.
- The light must give the impression that the light from the lantern is being reflected and magnified internally, and is coming out through the spotlight much brighter and more focused than before.
- The base of the light should have brackets or mounting holes so the prop can be easily secured to the concrete floor during installation.
- All materials used should be extremely sturdy (i.e. wood, metal pipes).
- The light must be enclosed inside a custom-built exterior housing, and it should not be able to move inside of its housing no matter how hard players shake it or strike it.
- The light housing must have handles on the left and right side that can be used to aim the spotlight.
- The light must be able to rotate at least 180 degrees and no more than 360 degrees on the vertical axis, and 45 degrees above and below horizontal.
# Technical Specifications
- [Must contain a DMX controllable pinspot light such as this one from ADJ](https://www.sweetwater.com/store/detail/PinLEDQDMX--adj-pinspot-led-quad-dmx)
- All connectors, buttons, and switches for the light must be easily accessible by installation and maintenance staff but totally inaccessible to players
- Must contain a reed switch that detects when the compartment door is closed
- Must contain several normally-closed reed switches wired in series to detect when a magnet is placed near the center of the compartment (this will trigger when the lantern is placed inside). These must be wired in series so that when any of them detect a magnet, the entire circuit is broken.
- The reed switches must be connected to an accessible female [4-pin JST connector such as these](https://a.co/d/0cV6Ah8y). We have extras of these and can provide them.
	- The door reed switch should connect to the black and green wires. The magnet reed switches should connect to the red and blue wires.
- All wires and connectors must reach the base and be accessible through a removable or openable maintenance panel that players cannot open.
- There must be a small, inconspicuous hole on the side of the base where the base touches the floor. We will be running cables into the base under a layer of astroturf on the floor directly through this hole.
